# Every view meets WCAG 2.2 level AA

Every view of the app must meet the success criteria of WCAG 2.2 at level A and level AA.

Reason: The app is a website of the Studierendenschaft, a public body, so § 10 L-BGG applies.
The law refers to EN 301 549, and EN 301 549 V4.1.1 refers to WCAG 2.2 AA.

Test: The rules in `NF/accessibility/`.
