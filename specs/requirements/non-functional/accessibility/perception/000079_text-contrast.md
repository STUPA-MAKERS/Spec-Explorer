# Text has a contrast of 4.5:1

Text must have a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 to its background. Text of 24 px, or bold
text of 18.66 px, must have at least 3:1. This applies in both themes and in the code excerpts.

Reason: WCAG 1.4.3.

Test: The axe check of 000074.
